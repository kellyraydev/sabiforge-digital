## Key corrections

**Big Beerah Collection is NOT a beverage brand.** Its supplied flyer shows hijabs, abayas, jersey scarves, necklaces, fabric bundles, ready-to-wear, gift boxes and everyday essentials. Use modest-fashion photography, soft blush, deep burgundy and gold. Do not use beverage images.

**Amzlat World of Beauty** is a mixed beauty, cosmetics, hair, fashion and accessories store according to its supplied website reference. Use the original pink/cream/black/gold visual identity rather than treating it as skincare-only.

**Opeyemi Phone & Gadgets** should stay blue/white with green WhatsApp CTAs. The new concept introduces clearer category navigation, a controlled sample-device display, an enquiry-first pattern and an accessible swap section. It uses locally cropped *illustrative/generated device visuals* and must be upgraded with approved high-resolution product photography before any public use.

## Concept-only, no false claims

All three are `Concept Project` presentations in SabiForge's ten-project portfolio; no client-project or live-inventory claims. **Do not copy phone numbers, addresses, business registration claims, prices, availability, warranties, shipping promises or social handles** from the supplied references into an active or new website without explicit business verification. The HTML prototype intentionally does not transmit enquiries, charge payments, display pricing or claim registered status.

The user-supplied screenshots may show real business logos and contacts. Keep them in design-review materials until the site owner authorizes their public use. The screenshot sources are not proof of a paid SabiForge client relationship.

## Quality checks performed

The standalone Opeyemi prototype was rendered and interaction-checked in headless Chromium from in-memory HTML. At widths 375, 390, 430, 768, 1280, 1440 and 1920 pixels: **no horizontal overflow or JavaScript page errors**; images loaded successfully when requested; filtering to Phones showed 2 sample results and resetting All showed 4; mobile menu opened. The preview uses a local modal for WhatsApp instead of a fake live link. This is prototype QA, not full production accessibility, performance, Safari or screen-reader verification.
